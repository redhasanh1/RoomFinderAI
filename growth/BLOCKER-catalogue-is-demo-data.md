## The part that kills outreach

Six listings are in **United States cities**:

    Quality 3-Bedroom Condo in New York
    Premium Apartment in New York
    Premium Condo in Los Angeles
    Premium 4-Bedroom House in Los Angeles
    Value 4-Bedroom House in San Jose
    1 bedroom — Evansville

Plus Vancouver, Calgary, Ottawa, Montreal — all outside the GTA target.

A Toronto landlord who gets our message, clicks through, and sees
"Premium Condo in Los Angeles" and "Modern condo perfect for tech
professionals" concludes one of two things: this is fake, or this is not
for me. Either way they never come back, and we only get one first
impression per landlord.

The titles are also visibly machine-generated in a bad way —
"Quality 3-Bedroom", "Premium", "Value" are adjectives no human landlord
writes. It reads as filler.

## Recommendation, in order

1. **Delete or unpublish the 6 US listings.** They serve no purpose and
   actively cost credibility. (Needs Hasan's OK — this is data deletion.)
2. Decide on Vancouver / Calgary / Ottawa / Montreal. Keeping them makes
   the site look national and thin. Cutting them makes it look local and
   focused. For a GTA student-housing pitch, focused wins.
3. What remains is ~5 Toronto + Waterloo + Hamilton listings, which is a
   credible "we are early, in your city" story rather than a fake one.
4. Then start outreach.

Being small and local is a fine story. Being fake is not, and right now
the catalogue reads as fake.
