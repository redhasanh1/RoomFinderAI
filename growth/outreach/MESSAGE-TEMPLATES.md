# Outreach templates — v1

Built on the research finding that the GTA is a renter's market in 2026
(1 in 19 rental units vacant, highest since 2021). The landlord's live pain
is an **empty room**, not writing an ad. Every template leads with that.

## Rules these follow

- **Never lead with "AI".** liv.rent and Airbnb already ship AI listing
  generation. It is not a differentiator and it makes us sound like a
  product looking for a problem.
- **Reference their actual listing.** One specific detail proves a human
  read it. This is the single biggest response-rate lever and the reason
  we send these one at a time.
- **One ask.** Not "sign up and post and share and follow."
- **Free, and say so twice.** There is no risk to weigh up.
- **Short.** Under 120 words. Landlords read on a phone between other jobs.
- **CASL:** every message identifies Hasan by name, gives a real reply
  address and a physical mailing address, and offers an explicit opt-out.
  These are legal requirements for a commercial electronic message in
  Canada, not politeness.

## A — Cold, to a landlord with a room advertised elsewhere

> **Subject: your room near [LANDMARK] — free listing on a local site**
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> I saw your [ROOM TYPE] on [WHERE] — the one [SPECIFIC DETAIL: "with the
> separate entrance" / "near Sheridan's Trafalgar campus"].
>
> I built RoomFinderAI, a free site for rooms in the GTA. It's new and it's
> small, but the students looking on it are looking specifically for rooms,
> not one-bedroom apartments — so your listing isn't competing with 400
> condos.
>
> Listing is free, takes about two minutes, and you keep your ad wherever
> else it is. If nobody messages you, you've lost two minutes.
>
> Want me to put it up for you? Send me the photos and I'll do it — you
> just confirm it's right.
>
> Hasan
> RoomFinderAI · roomfinderai.com
> [MAILING ADDRESS]
> Reply STOP and I won't contact you again.

**Why this one should win:** the closing offer removes 100% of the work.
We are not asking them to sign up and fill eight fields — we are offering
to do it. At low volume that is affordable, and it converts far better
than any incentive we could name.

## B — Cold, leading with the market

> **Subject: rooms are sitting empty in [AREA] right now**
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> Nearly 1 in 19 rental units in the GTA is vacant at the moment — the
> highest since 2021, mostly because of the cap on international students.
> If your room has been up a while, that's why, and it isn't you.
>
> I run RoomFinderAI, a free room-listing site for the GTA. Adding another
> channel costs you nothing and takes two minutes.
>
> Happy to post it for you if you'd rather — just send photos.
>
> Hasan
> RoomFinderAI · roomfinderai.com
> [MAILING ADDRESS]
> Reply STOP and I won't contact you again.

**Test against A.** B demonstrates we understand their market. Risk: it
opens by telling them something discouraging.

## What NOT to send

- Anything with "revolutionary", "AI-powered", "game-changing"
- Anything promising tenants. We cannot deliver that yet and a landlord
  who gets nothing after being promised something never comes back.
- The same text twice. If it reads as a template it is a template.
- Anything to a landlord whose ad says "no agents" or "no soliciting" —
  that removes the CASL implied-consent basis entirely.

## Tracking

Every send logged in `growth/data/outreach-log.csv` — who, where their ad
was, which template, date, reply, outcome. Without this we cannot tell
which template works and the whole exercise is guesswork.
