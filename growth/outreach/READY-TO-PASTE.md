# Ready to paste — one per group

Each is written for the specific group, not copy-pasted. Groups with 10+ posts
a day need short and scannable; small community groups need warmer and longer.
Posting near-identical text across groups is what Facebook's spam detection
looks for, so these deliberately differ in wording, length and opening.

**Post one. Wait for replies. Then the next one the following day.**
Not four in an hour.

---

## Before posting, every time

- **Message the admin first.** *"Hi, is it okay if I share a free
  room-listing site I built? Happy not to post if it's not allowed."*
  Admins ban links, not people who asked.
- **Reply to every comment within the hour.** At this size your response time
  is the product.
- **Log it** in `growth/data/outreach-log.csv`.

## When somebody asks "how many listings do you have?"

Tell them the truth:

> Honestly not many yet — that's exactly why I'm posting. Yours would be free
> and I'll do the work of putting it up.

Someone who lists on a nearly-empty site because you were straight with them
sticks around. Someone who lists because you oversold it leaves and says so.
