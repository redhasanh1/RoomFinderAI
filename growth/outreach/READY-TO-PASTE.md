## Register, first

Feedback on the first live post (Brampton Rentals, 2026-09-02): **too corny.**
It had a rhetorical-question hook, quoted vacancy statistics at a landlord who
lives that vacancy every day, and used "I'm not going to pretend otherwise",
which is itself a copywriting move. Five paragraphs to make one offer.

The register of these groups is the post that sat directly underneath it:

> Hi i am boy looking for a private room in brampton from 1st november.
> *i work full time * need car parking * i am vegetarian

Blunt, functional, no rhetoric. Match that. Rules:

- **Three lines, four at most.** If it needs a second paragraph to explain
  itself, it is an advert.
- **No opening question.** "Rooms sitting empty?" is an infomercial.
- **No statistics.** They know the market better than we do. Quoting CMHC at
  them is condescending.
- **No self-aware honesty.** "I won't pretend it's big" performs humility.
  Just be small and say nothing about it.
- **No emoji unless the group uses them heavily.**
- Say the thing, make the offer, stop.

## When someone asks how many listings there are

Say the number.

> Not many yet. Yours would be free and I'll do the posting.

Do not dress it up. Someone who lists because you were straight with them
stays; someone who lists because you oversold leaves and says so in the group.

## Before every post

- **Check who owns the group.** "Rooms For Rent Mississauga, CA" turned out
  to be run by RoomStarts.com, a competitor - its banner says so. Posting a
  rival site there gets removed and probably banned. Check the cover image and
  the About section first, every time.
- **One group, then wait.** Not four in an hour.
- **Reply to every comment fast.** At this size, response time is the product.
- **Log it** in growth/data/outreach-log.csv.
