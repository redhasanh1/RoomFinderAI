# WhatsApp / community group kit

The GTA room market runs through community WhatsApp and Facebook groups —
Brampton, Malton, Mississauga, Scarborough, Rexdale. Landlords with a
basement and students needing a room are in the *same* chat. That is the
channel.

**Hasan posts these himself, in groups he is already a member of.** That is a
member sharing something useful, not solicitation — no CASL issue, no
platform ToS issue. Nobody should be cold-DMing strangers from a scraped
member list; that is spam and it gets the number banned.

---

## Rules for this channel specifically

- **Ask the admin first.** One line: *"bhaji, can I share a free room-listing
  site I built? happy to not post if it's not allowed."* Admins ban links,
  not people who ask.
- **Never paste the same text into ten groups the same hour.** WhatsApp's
  spam detection is link-and-frequency based. That is how a number gets
  restricted.
- **One message. No follow-up blast.** If it lands, people reply in-thread.
- **Do not post a Play Store link until the app is actually live.** It is in
  review now. A dead link is the worst possible first impression, and you
  only get one post per group.
- **Reply to every single reply, fast.** In these groups your response time
  IS the product. Somebody asking "does it work in Brampton?" at 11pm and
  getting an answer at 11:02pm is worth more than any feature.

## A — Landlord-facing, general group

> Assalamualaikum / Hi everyone 👋
>
> I built a free site for renting out rooms in the GTA — roomfinderai.com
>
> If you have a room or basement sitting empty, you can put it up free. No
> fee, no commission, and you keep your ad on Kijiji or wherever else it
> already is.
>
> It's new and it's small, I'm not going to pretend otherwise. But it's free
> and it takes 2 minutes.
>
> If you don't want to fill in a form, send me the photos and the address and
> I'll put it up for you — you just check it before it goes live.

**Why it reads right:** admits it's small (everyone can see that anyway, and
pretending otherwise kills trust instantly), removes all risk, and the last
line removes all the work.

## B — Student / tenant-facing group

> Hi all 👋 I built a free site for finding rooms in the GTA —
> roomfinderai.com
>
> It's rooms only, not full apartments, so you're not scrolling past condos
> you can't afford.
>
> Still building it up, so if you don't see your area yet give it a bit. And
> if you're a landlord in here — putting a room up is free.

**This one does double duty.** Every student group has landlords lurking in
it. The last line is aimed at them.

---

## C — When somebody replies "does it actually have listings?"

Do not oversell. Say:

> Honestly not many yet — that's why I'm posting. Putting yours up is free
> and takes 2 min, and I'll do it for you if you send photos.

Someone who lists on a nearly-empty site because you were straight with them
becomes an actual supporter. Someone who lists because you oversold it leaves
and tells people.

---

## D — Once the app is approved (not yet — it is in review)

> The app's on the Play Store now if it's easier than the site: [LINK]
>
> Free to post a room, same as the site.

Apps share better than websites in WhatsApp — the Play Store link previews
with an icon and installs in one tap. That is the version of this to lead
with **once it is live**, and it is worth going back to the groups for.

## Track every post

Log each one in `growth/data/outreach-log.csv`: which group, roughly how
many members, which message, date, replies, listings that came from it.
Without that we cannot tell which groups are worth going back to, and this
channel lives or dies on knowing that.
