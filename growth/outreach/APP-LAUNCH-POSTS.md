## Timing

These groups run on **evening traffic**. A post at 8am is buried by the time
anyone reads. Best window is roughly **6–9pm local**, when people who spent the
day at work sit down and scroll.

## The one thing worth mentioning that we could not say before

The app does something the site does not: **photos in, and it writes the title
and description and suggests a price.** That is the only line in any of this
that describes something a landlord cannot get on Kijiji.

Use it sparingly and never as "AI" — say what it does, not what it is.
