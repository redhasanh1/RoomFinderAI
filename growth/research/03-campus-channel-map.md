# The campus channel — this is the road to 1,000 landlords

The single most useful finding of the night. Schools do not just tolerate
third-party housing platforms; they **replace their own boards with them**,
and there is a documented playbook for how a vendor gets in.

## Proof that schools replace in-house boards with a vendor

- **Brock publishes a "transition to Places4Students" page** — a school
  publicly documenting that it retired its own listing board for a third
  party.
- **Places4Students' UTSU deal was signed by Hank Ko, AVP External, in
  Dec 2014** — a named signing officer at a students' union.
- **No students' union in the whole set runs its own listing board** —
  not SSU, IGNITE, YFS, SSF, UTSU, TMSU, USC, or GBC SA. The union is the
  *signing party*, not the operator. That is the door.

  winner-takes-all.

  Treat McMaster as a **paid competitor**, not a free link list. Our free

## Why this beats messaging landlords one at a time

A landlord found via a campus housing office arrives with the school's
implied endorsement. There is no ToS, no solicitation clause, no
liquidated-damages schedule, and no CASL consent problem — the landlord
comes to us. One relationship can carry dozens of landlords, and it renews
every intake.
