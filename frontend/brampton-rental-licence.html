If you rent out a room or a basement in Brampton, you now need a licence

Since 1 January 2026 the Residential Rental Licensing programme covers the whole city and every rental property with one to four units. The licence itself is free. Not having one can cost up to $1,500.

The short version

It's free

The City's own words: licence applications and renewals are free. There is no fee to get one and no fee to renew it.

It's mandatory

One to four residential units, anywhere in Brampton, licensed before operating. Not a formality you catch up on later.

It's slow right now

The licensing office says it is taking roughly 12 weeks to review applications because of the volume. Start early.

Which properties need one

The City lists three property types, and between them they cover almost every way a person rents out part of a house in Brampton:

  • Single dwelling units — the whole residence, no additional units.
  • Two-unit dwellings — your principal residence plus one additional residential unit. This is the classic basement apartment.
  • Multi-unit residential dwellings — more than one additional residential unit, including triplexes, fourplexes and garden suites.

The programme is city-wide since January 2026 — it is no longer limited to particular wards — and the City says it runs until 31 December 2028.

The education module catches people out

Since January 2026, every applicant must complete a one-time online educational module. Once, not every year — but until it is done the application is not finished, and with a twelve-week queue behind it that is not a step to discover late.

Conditions covering property maintenance, occupancy, insurance and compliance are attached to every licence, and Enforcement and By-law Services inspect against them.

What it costs to skip it

The City says failing to apply for a licence or comply with the programme can result in fines and penalties, and that non-compliant rental properties can face penalties of up to $1,500. Set against a licence that costs nothing, there is no version of this where not applying is the cheaper choice.

Straight answers

Do I need a licence to rent out a room or basement in Brampton?

If you rent out one to four residential units in Brampton, yes. Since 1 January 2026 the programme is city-wide and covers single dwelling units, two-unit dwellings with one additional residential unit, and multi-unit dwellings including triplexes, fourplexes and garden suites. The licence must be obtained before operating.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The City states that licence applications and renewals are free. The licence is annual, so it has to be renewed, but the renewal is free as well.

What happens if I don't get one?

The City says failing to apply or comply can result in fines and penalties, that Enforcement and By-law Services will inspect for compliance, and that non-compliant rental properties can face penalties of up to $1,500.

Is there a course I have to take?

Yes. Since 1 January 2026 all applicants must complete a one-time online educational module. It is once, not annually.

How long does it take?

Longer than you would expect. In September 2026 the rental licensing office's own auto-reply said that due to a high number of renewal and new applications it is taking approximately 12 weeks to review applications. Apply well before you need the room filled.
