Delete Your Account

RoomFinderAI · Last Updated: 26 August 2026

Delete it from the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes about thirty seconds.

  1. Open the RoomFinderAI app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom tab bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is closed immediately.

Delete it by email

If you cannot get into the app — a lost password, a phone you no longer have — email us and we will do it for you.

  • Write to support@roomfinderai.com from the email address on the account.
  • Put “Delete my account” in the subject line.
  • We verify that the request came from the account holder, then delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your name, email address and profile details
  • Your account credentials
  • Room listings you posted, and the photos attached to them
  • Your roommate and sublease posts
  • Your saved and favourited rooms
  • Your conversations with the AI negotiator

What is kept, and why

  • Messages you sent to other people stay in the other person's inbox, shown without your profile attached. We cannot delete something from someone else's conversation, in the same way an email you have already sent stays in the recipient's mailbox.
  • Records we are required to keep — anything we must retain to comply with the law, resolve a dispute, or enforce our terms — are kept for as long as that obligation lasts and are then deleted.
  • Backups. Deleted data can survive in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before those backups roll over. It is not accessible in the product during that time.

Deleting some of your data without closing your account

You do not have to delete the whole account to remove things. In the app you can delete an individual listing, a roommate or sublease post, or a conversation, and keep the account itself. If you want a specific piece of data removed and cannot find how, email support@roomfinderai.com and ask.

This cannot be undone

Deleting your account is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account, its listings or its messages, and signing up again with the same email address creates a new, empty account.

Questions

Email support@roomfinderai.com. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how it is handled.
