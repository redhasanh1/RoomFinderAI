The single most-asked question about a room, and the one most ads leave out. How many housemates, students or working, quiet or social, any house rules. Answering it up front cuts out the viewings that were never going to work.

Straight answers

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no listing fee, no commission on the rent, and no time limit on the ad.

Why list here as well?

Because this is the only place your room is not competing against condos and whole apartments. Every listing here is a room, so the people browsing are looking for exactly what you have. Listing takes two minutes and costs nothing.

Do I have to take it down elsewhere?

No. Keep it on every site you are already using. This is one more channel, not a replacement.

What happens to my details?

Renters message you through the site — your email is not published on the listing. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I take the listing down?

Any time, from your profile, in one tap. Once the room is rented you delete it and you are done.
